# AQ-ADR-005 — Signal ordering and cursor

- **Status:** Accepted.
- **Decide before:** `AQ-05`
- **Contract:** `AQ-CONT-1`
- **Invariants:** AQ-H5
- **Architecture references:** §9.6, §9.8, §10.3, §10.6 of
  [`actionqueue-hardening-implementation-ready.md`](../contracts/actionqueue-hardening-implementation-ready.md)

## Context

A wait may be established before or after a matching signal arrives. Without a
durable ordering, early signals are lost or duplicated across restart.

## Recommended decision

Assign a monotonic per-store `SignalSequence` at admission. Every `WaitSpec` carries a
lower-bound cursor; `After(s)` is exclusive: under `FirstMatch` the lowest eligible sequence strictly greater than `s` wins.
The WAL commit order of the wait and the signal is the sole tie-breaker.

## Alternatives considered

Wall-clock timestamps (rejected: not monotonic across restart); per-namespace
sequences (deferred: adds index complexity without a first-release need).

## Consequences

Signals are fan-out observations, not consumed queue items (§9.7). Cursor
semantics are part of the durable record model.

## Verification required

No-lost-wakeup matrix (§28.4): signal-before-wait, wait-before-signal, duplicate
signal, restart between each step.

## AQ-05 implementation decisions

`SignalSequence` is a checked, global per-store counter starting at one. It is
independent of WAL sequence and producer/receipt timestamps. Duplicates allocate
neither sequence. Records also store their admitting WAL sequence. AQ-08 compound
frames can allocate consecutive signal sequences in persisted proposal order.

The only deduplication identity is `(Option<TenantId>, SignalId)`. CanonicalSignalV1
uses SHA-256 of domain-separated explicit bytes: tenant, id, namespace, kind,
correlation, causation, source, complete payload representation, effective payload
hash, occurrence time. Options and variants have explicit tags; text/bytes are
u64-LE length-prefixed; UUIDs use their 16 bytes. The domain is
`AQ-CONT-1\0signal\0`, followed by u32-LE version 1. SHA-256 is hash tag 1.
Receipt time, control attribution and assigned sequences are excluded.

Inline bytes are hash-verified. Omitted envelope hashes normalize from payloads;
conflicting hashes reject. Hash-only observations are permitted. External locators
are never fetched. A retry preserves the first receipt and attribution, including
a retry after retirement. New scoped signals require an existing tenant and the
platform store profile. Local task/run/attempt ancestry must exist and agree; opaque
external references grant no permission. Authentication remains the host's job.

Storage owns ordered tenant and exact namespace/kind/correlation/source indexes.
Query cursors are exclusive; page sizes clamp to 1,024 (zero returns no results).
Candidates are ordered, non-consuming reads. `AnyRetained` still requires correlation
when constructing a wait. Wait establishment/resolution remains AQ-06.
